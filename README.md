# Courtyard

Courtyard is a playable faction for VCMI, inspired by Baroque architecture and alchemy. Its army combines animated statues, alchemical creatures, gunpowder troops and fantastic beasts.

## Features

- Seven creature tiers with 15 recruitable creatures, including two alternative Dodo upgrades.
- 16 heroes with unique specialties.
- Two special war machines: Camera Obscura and Potion Cart.
- Unique creature abilities, town buildings and adventure map dwellings.
- Expanded Stack Experience with distinct development paths for basic and upgraded creatures.

Courtyard offers a mix of defensive tactics, magic support, ranged combat and summoned creatures.
